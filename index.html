My work connects the possibilities of AI with the realities of education and research — translating complex technologies into useful, responsible and human-centred practice.

01 — ABOUT

A bridge between technology and people.

I am an AI and language technology professional with a background spanning computational linguistics, humanities research and university teaching.

My experience includes working with generative AI, natural language processing, speech technologies and multilingual workflows, alongside developing learning resources and supporting the adoption of AI tools.

Having studied and worked in Germany, France and Canada, I bring an international perspective and a strong interest in how higher education institutions can approach AI with curiosity, critical thinking and responsibility.

I am particularly interested in roles that connect strategy and implementation: helping institutions develop digital capabilities, strengthen AI literacy and create meaningful learning experiences.
